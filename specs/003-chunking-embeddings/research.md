## Official OpenAI facts and gaps

**VERIFIED from official docs:** [embeddings API reference](https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/resources/embeddings/methods/create): `/v1/embeddings`; nonempty input, 8,192 tokens per input, at most 2,048 inputs and 300,000 tokens per request; optional `dimensions` on text-embedding-3+, `float`/`base64` output, indexed embeddings and usage. [Embeddings guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/embeddings): text-embedding-3-small default 1,536 dimensions. [FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6824809): OpenAI embeddings are L2 normalized, including shortened vectors. [Official model page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/text-embedding-3-small) displays $0.02 per 1M input tokens, but the [general pricing page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing) did not expose an embedding entry in the fetched text; pricing-page confirmation is **NOT VERIFIED**. Treat the CLI cost as an estimate. [Rate-limit guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/rate-limits) names `Retry-After` and `x-ratelimit-limit-requests`, `-limit-tokens`, `-remaining-requests`, `-remaining-tokens`, `-reset-requests`, `-reset-tokens` (plus project-token variants), and says temporary 429/503 may carry `Retry-After`. [Error guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/error-codes) distinguishes transient 429 from quota/billing 429; 500 may be retried.
